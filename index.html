Green read prototype

A 20 m square of green at true scale, with slopes of about 1–4%. Lit from straight overhead on purpose, so the bare surface honestly reads flat. Every overlay and the putt solver read from the same height function and its analytic gradient.

  • Read toggles the whole overlay. That contrast is the point.
  • Roll it replays the solved putt on its real clock. Watch distance and break diverge: most of the break happens late.
  • Pace changes the line. Firmer takes less break.
  • Tap to move the ball or hole. Vertical scale exaggerates the picture only, never the physics.
